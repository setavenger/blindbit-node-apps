Privacy

Anyone who can reach port {{electrum_tls_port}} on your local network, or who knows the onion address, can read this wallet's addresses, transactions and balance. Do not forward these ports on your router.

Friglet gets tweak data from the BlindBit oracle (oracle.setor.dev), a third-party service run by BlindBit. The operator sees this node's IP address, which block ranges are scanned and when. It never sees your keys.

Uninstalling Friglet deletes /mnt/hdd/mynode/blindbit-friglet: the scan key, the wallet settings, the scan progress and the login password. Your bitcoin is not affected.
